# Changelog

Each production release has a version and a dated entry below. Newest releases appear first.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-22

- Open a draft from a Siri Shortcut and review it in Quick Expense before saving.
- Transcribe voice commands on your device with Whisper after downloading the speech model. Voice entry requires an internet connection.
- Accept more spoken and typed patterns, including “I spent $40 on groceries” and “$40 in Walmart groceries.”

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-21

- Log an expense from an English voice or typed command, then review and edit it before saving.
- Read the Google OAuth client ID from GitHub's production secret or variable so the live build can offer Drive and email sign-in.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-21

- Plan monthly income, expenses, recurring bills, and savings, and compare the plan with recorded transactions.
- Track accounts, transfers, debt, and spending trends. Import bank CSVs and export Excel workbooks and PDF statements.
- Back up and restore Ledger data, optionally save to Google Drive, and install Ledger for offline use.
- Personalize Ledger with eight palettes, seven font options, and six interface languages, including Arabic right-to-left navigation.
